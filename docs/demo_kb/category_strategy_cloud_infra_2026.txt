JET INDUSTRIES — APPROVED CATEGORY STRATEGY
Category: Cloud Infrastructure & Cloud Professional Services
Document type: category_strategy
Version: 2026.1
Approved: 18 March 2026
Owner: Category Management / Strategic Sourcing
Next formal review: December 2026

1. BUSINESS INTENT
Jet Industries will treat cloud infrastructure and related professional services as a strategic category.
The category must protect continuity of production workloads, reduce uncontrolled rate inflation, and give
business stakeholders a clear view of concentration and renewal risk.

2. CATEGORY OBJECTIVES
- Keep production cloud platforms available without single-supplier lock-in for critical workloads.
- Hold professional-services cloud rates within ±10% of approved category medians unless an exception is logged.
- Bring every Tier-1 / Tier-2 cloud agreement into the renewal pipeline at least six months before expiry.
- Prefer suppliers that can show FinOps, security posture, and residency packaging with cited evidence.

3. STRATEGIC ASSUMPTIONS
A1. Acme Cloud Services remains the incumbent for core infrastructure through 31 December 2026
    (contract ACME-MSA-2024-001).
A2. CloudCo Systems remains the preferred challenger for FinOps and EU residency packaging.
A3. Annual cloud professional-services spend stays under $4.2M unless a board-approved growth case is opened.
A4. Business stakeholders will accept a dual-supplier posture when concentration risk exceeds 60% of category spend.
A5. No material change in EU AI / data-training restrictions will force a mid-cycle renegotiation in 2026.

4. SOURCING APPROACH
- Incumbent management: Acme for core IaaS / managed cloud with commercial challenge at renewal.
- Challenger path: CloudCo for FinOps / security posture work under $500K ACV.
- Rate card discipline: normalize titles against the Jet job taxonomy before approving T&M engagements.
- Decision cadence: quarterly Category Strategy Intelligence Briefing to the Cloud SteerCo.

5. KNOWN RISKS
- Auto-renewal and notice windows on the Acme MSA can trap spend if the briefing cycle slips.
- Me-too positioning between Acme and challengers can hide real FinOps differentiation.
- Unmapped rate-card titles create false savings claims.

6. OPEN DECISIONS CARRIED FROM PRIOR CYCLE
- Whether to open a competitive renegotiation with Acme before the 2026 renewal window.
- Whether CloudCo should be elevated from tactical FinOps to a secondary production support path.
- Whether a category-level rate ceiling should be published to hiring managers for cloud engineering roles.

7. SUCCESS MEASURES FOR THIS STRATEGY
- Zero Tier-1 cloud renewals entered with less than 90 days remaining notice.
- At least one evidenced challenger option presented for every material Acme rate variance above 15%.
- Stakeholder briefing cycle time under five business days from evidence close to published brief.
