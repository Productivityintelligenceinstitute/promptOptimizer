STATEMENT OF WORK
Document ID: NW-SOW-2025-008
Title: Billing export rebuild
Customer: Northwind Digital Ltd
Supplier: Harborline Consulting Ltd
Governing agreement: NW-MSA-2025-003
Status: Completed 18 November 2025
Commercial model: Fixed deliverable

1. Outcome
Harborline will replace the legacy billing export with a versioned CSV export that Finance can reconcile to the general ledger without manual rekeying.

2. Deliverable
Deliverable: Billing export v2.
What: A production CSV export of closed invoices for a selected month.
Who produces it: Harborline delivery lead.
Who accepts it: Northwind Finance Operations Manager.
When: Acceptance review within 10 business days of delivery.
Where: Northwind production billing environment.
Why: Month-end close currently rekeys invoice totals.
How accepted: Finance reconciles three sample months to the ledger with zero unexplained differences.
Supplier control: Harborline controls design, build, and deployment. Northwind provides read-only data access and the acceptance review.

3. Payment
40 percent on delivery of the export to the test environment.
60 percent on written acceptance.
No monthly capacity fee.

4. What this SOW does not do
This Statement of Work does not restate liability, confidentiality, or governing law. Those terms stay in NW-MSA-2025-003.
