JET INDUSTRIES PROCUREMENT POLICY
Version: 2.1
Effective: March 1, 2025
Owner: Corporate Procurement

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines mandatory controls for sourcing, contracting, renewals, and supplier onboarding across Jet Industries.

2. MANDATORY CONTRACT CLAUSES
All supplier agreements above $50,000 annual spend must include:
- Scope of services and acceptance criteria
- Payment terms, invoicing cadence, and audit rights
- Confidentiality and data protection obligations
- Information security and incident notification (max 72 hours)
- Subprocessor approval and flow-down requirements
- AI/data-use restrictions and training prohibitions where applicable
- Liability caps, indemnities, and insurance minimums
- Termination, transition assistance, and exit obligations
- Renewal notice, auto-renewal opt-out, and price-change mechanics
- Governing law and dispute resolution

3. RENEWAL LEAD TIMES
- Low complexity: alert at 3 months before expiry
- Medium complexity: alert at 6 months before expiry
- High complexity / strategic: alert at 9–12 months before expiry

4. SUPPLIER RISK TIERS
- Tier 1 (strategic): board-visible categories, multi-region dependency
- Tier 2 (important): material spend or regulated data processing
- Tier 3 (tactical): commodity / low-risk services

5. RATE CARD GOVERNANCE
Professional services engagements must use an approved rate card or competitive benchmark within ±10% of category medians unless an exception is documented.

6. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
Exceptions require Procurement Director approval and must be logged with residual risk and expiry date.
